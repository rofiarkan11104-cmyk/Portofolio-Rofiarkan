About Me

Who's Rofi' Arkan?

I’m someone who believes that life isn’t just about where we’re going, but also about who we get to walk with along the way. I love cooking, exploring new places, camping under the night sky, or simply watching the sunset when everything feels a little quieter.

I want to collect as many stories as I can in this life — about the journeys, the laughter, the places I’ve never been to, and those little moments that might seem simple but somehow stay with you. Because sometimes, happiness comes from the things we never planned for.

And maybe, somewhere along all the journeys I’ll take, there’ll be someone who comes into my life without much of a plan, and slowly becomes the most beautiful part of the story. Not someone perfect, just someone who makes the long journey feel a little warmer and makes me realize that maybe home isn’t always a place. Sometimes, home is a person.
